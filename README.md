# Journey Through the Stars!
**Journey Through the Stars!** is a Top-down Puzzle RPG made in GameMaker Studio. you play as Cedric! the Vampire Mage who doesn't know magic(yet), and has to go through multiple obstacles to reach the wizard's tower and bring him a Star.

## Controls
Use The **arrow keys** to move around.

Interact and advance text with **Z**

Skip text with **X**

Sprint with **Left Shift**
